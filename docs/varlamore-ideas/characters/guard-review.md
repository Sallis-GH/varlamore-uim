# Guard (Fortis Cothon) — script review

Wiki checked: Guard (Varlamore), Kualti, Knight of Varlamore, Fortis Cothon, Transcript:Border Guard (Varlamore), Dizana's quiver.

Facts confirmed: Guards are present at the Cothon (Civitas illa Fortis spawns, 52); the Kualti are the Queen's elite guard, HQ beside the palace, entered after years of army service plus knight training — so an ordinary guard envying their kit is sound. Dizana's quiver is visible in the cape slot and is "awarded to those who have proved their worth in the Colosseum", so a harbour guard recognising it is fair. The Cothon has spice, gem, fur, silk and baker stalls and The Flaming Arrow. Nothing on the wiki mentions a crane or a ledger desk (invented, but harmless). The Border Guard polices entry into Varlamore on the Queen's arrangements — nothing anywhere bans sailing out, and charter ships leave the Cothon freely.

## Pass rates

**Writer A — 37/43 = 86%.** Sounds like the game: clipped, procedural, no poetry, and the "crates have permission, you don't" beat is the best single line in either draft; it loses points for one invented crane, a cold-weather joke on a savannah harbour, and a post variant that turns into a set of directions.

**Writer B — 19/24 = 79%.** The register is right and the brush-offs land, but it hangs the whole gate on the Queen's word, which is the one framing the wiki contradicts, and the drafts are thin — variant 1 is four lines and three options.

## Challenges

1. **Both writers let him explain the rule.** The brief says he takes orders, gives none, and knows the rule but not why. A explains the standard ("Nobody sails out unproven"), B explains the authority ("The Queen's word"). The approved version never states a rule at all — "Not for you." / "Next question." That's the character. I'd have him refuse flatly and make the player go find out why from someone else; the option branch that asks "who decides?" is where a name is allowed to drop, once.

2. **The post variant became a signpost.** A gives dock building, desk on the left, ask for the ledger, and wear the quiver in — four instructions across three branches. B says follow the ledger, which doesn't mean anything. The approved post variant is one word: "Fine." I'd keep the whole reaction to a grunt plus "Registrar's inside", and let the player ask if they want more. Layering guidance is exactly the hint-forcing the owner rejected.

3. **The Crown is the wrong authority, and the human touches are the wrong warmth.** Nothing on the wiki restricts leaving Varlamore — the Border Guard checks people coming *in*, and charter ships sail out of the Cothon all day. Pinning it on the Queen makes the plugin's premise a false law instead of a personal reputation problem; it should be "no captain will take you and nobody will vouch". Separately, A gives him a long shift, sore feet, armour envy, and a wish to see the Colosseum. Pick one. A guard who volunteers his feelings isn't funny; the joke is a man who won't give you a spare syllable, and the Kualti armour line is the only crack worth keeping.
