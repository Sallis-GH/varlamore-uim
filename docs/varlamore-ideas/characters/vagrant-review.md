# Vagrant (Sunset Coast pier) — script review

## Fact-check notes (wiki, fetched)

- **Sunset Coast** is a Fremennik village in the Avium Savannah, "just south-west of the Hunter Guild", reached by quetzal, charter ship, Antonia's boat from Aldarin, or a personal boat at 44 Sailing. It has ovens, two anvils in Thurid's shop, Picaria's fishing shop, a **sand pit at the northern end**, and **shark** fishing spots (big net / harpoon). Both drafts' scraps, ovens, gulls and rope are safe.
- **Charter ships** are Trader Stan's network; players board by talking to a trader crewmember and picking a port. No draft quotes a fare, so no fare error. Nothing in the network implies a crew may refuse a passenger — the refusal has to sit with the harbour, not the fleet.
- **Antonia** runs the Sunset Coast–Aldarin boat for **20 coins** (free with Ring of Charos(a)). She is the one boat a pier drifter would certainly know by name, and **neither draft mentions her**. See Challenge 1.
- **Fortis Cothon** is "a large harbour on the north side of Civitas illa Fortis"; the capital sits north of the Avium Savannah, with the **Colosseum on the cliffs east of the city**. There is no documented coast road from Sunset Coast to the Cothon — the route is inland across the savannah. Writer B's "coast road" fails on this.
- **Dizana's quiver** is awarded for defeating Sol Heredit in the Colosseum and is worn visibly. "Colosseum prize" is accurate.
- **Begging**: the only wiki source is a Wealthy citizen snapping "No! We don't care for begging in the city!" — that contempt is a *Civitas illa Fortis* civic attitude, not a Sunset Coast one. Neither draft has him beg, which is correct; a Fremennik fishing village has no documented position on vagrancy, so his life here is safely invented.
- **Local words**: he stepped off a charter ship, so he is a foreigner and must never say Nilsal/iknami/Tetamo. Neither draft does. Correct.

## Challenges

**1. Boots are a tic, not a character — and the one real fact about this pier went unused.**
Between them the drafts say "boots" eleven times. B's second variant is *about* the boots being all he has. Meanwhile Antonia runs a boat from this very dock to Aldarin for 20 coins, and neither writer touched it. That is the joke the brief was sitting on: he *can* get you a boat. It is the wrong boat, it costs twenty coins, and it goes to Aldarin, which is still Varlamore. Give him one boots observation per conversation and let the second thing he knows be Antonia.

**2. Both writers made him sad. He should be comfortable.**
"Water's cold and the pier's dry." "Every deckhand who never made it home." "There's nothing here." Three separate lines invite the player to feel something about his life, and every one of them is where the register breaks. The approved Guard is funny because nothing is explained and nobody is asking for sympathy. He stepped off a ship and stayed because it is cool under the pier and the ovens throw out scraps at dusk. Cut every line that comments on his own situation and the voice fixes itself.

**3. The gate is either over-explained or absent, and one line edges toward a ban.**
A tells the player how it works twice, in the pre phase, in a conversation with a man who admits he only hears the shouting. B never mentions it at all across two pre variants, so the post variant's advice arrives from nowhere. Neither is right. He should be confidently wrong: he thinks it is the crews sizing you up, because that is all he can see from under a pier. Then "bloke with the ledger" in the post variant is genuinely new. On framing — the wiki has no outbound ban, and A's "The harbour won't" is the closest either draft comes to sounding like one. Keep it as one unproven stranger nobody will vouch for ("Charter crews want a name on you"), never as a rule about everyone.
