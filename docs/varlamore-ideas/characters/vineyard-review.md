## Challenges

**1. The worker is male, and both drafts forgot.**
The wiki's Vineyard worker is a male NPC, examine "Looks after the grapes." writer-a writes "she" in all three premises; writer-b writes "she", then "he", then "she" again, and ships a narration box that says "She checks her list". If the stand-in reuses the Vineyard worker model, it is a he. I would fix the pronoun rather than the model: the male grape-picker with a bad opinion of boats is the funnier version anyway, and it costs nothing.

**2. The brief hands every writer the same prop, and they both took it.**
"Sent down to the dock to watch a wine shipment" produces crates, a tally, and a list, so both drafts opened on counting crates and both ended on "mind the gulls". The dock is not what makes this character funny. What makes him funny is that he is the only person at a harbour who has no opinion about ships. I would rewrite the brief so he is at the dock for a reason with friction in it: Mistrock supplies the winery with metal at a cut rate, so send him down to meet the dwarves' cart and let him be annoyed about that instead. Then the ship question is an interruption, not his job.

**3. Both drafts made a grape-picker explain the sailing rule.**
writer-b says "Nobody sails till the crown says so" and "Something about proving yourself first"; writer-a says "Something about the capital saying no". This is the thing the owner rejected twice over: it is hand-holding, and in writer-b's case it also invents a kingdom-wide ban the wiki does not support. The approved Guard works because of what it refuses to say. A vineyard hand should not know the rule at all. He should know that no captain wants the bother, and then go back to the crates. Save the registrar for the post variant, where the brief actually asks for it, and give the pre variants the wine instead: he can name Eclipse red, he can resent the foreman, he can note that the influential people own the vines. None of the six pre options in either draft use the one subject this character is an authority on.
