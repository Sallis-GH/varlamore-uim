## Challenges

**1. The brief says he "has no idea how the Kingdom works" — the wiki says otherwise, and both writers lost material to it.**
Sunset Coast's own page: the Fremenniks "travelled from Rellekka to Varlamore to explore
the recently opened up kingdom and fight in its colosseum." His neighbours went to the
arena. He would know exactly what that cape is, and would have an opinion about the ones
who went. Writer B stumbled into this correctly ("Colosseum-make") without being allowed
to use it. **Instead:** brief him as the one Fremennik who *didn't* go — he stayed for the
fish. That is a whole character in one sentence, and it turns the post variant into a
reaction to a neighbour's choice rather than to a stranger's accessory.

**2. Both writers made him grumpy, and grumpy is not a joke.**
"Don't talk to me outerlander" and "Go bother someone else" are the same beat five times
across the two drafts. The approved Guard works because "Next question." refuses to engage
with the premise, not because it is rude. **Instead:** make him incurious rather than
hostile. He isn't refusing to take the player out — it hasn't occurred to him that "out"
is a place a person goes. "Out where?" is the funniest line on this dock and Writer A
spent it in line two of a variant he then filled with weather.

**3. Nobody should be telling the player where the capital is, least of all this man.**
Writer A gives three options that all point at the registrar, and gets the compass and the
coastline wrong doing it. Writer B has him name Fortis Cothon by its proper name, having
never been. **Instead:** the post variant should hand over one line and no directions —
"Take it to the harbour in the capital. Not to me." — and let his ignorance be the joke
when the player presses. "I've never been. Too far from the water." is funnier and safer
than a bearing.

**Minor:** the brief's "sells to Picaria's shop" is not supported — her shop buys sardine
through swordfish and lobster, no shark. Sunset Coast has cooking ovens; send the catch there.
