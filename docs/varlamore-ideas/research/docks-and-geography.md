# Varlamore Charter Docks & Geography — Wiki Research

Source of truth: OSRS Wiki (oldschool.runescape.wiki), fetched live. Every fact below is
cited to the page it came from. Quotes kept under 125 characters.

## 1. The Three Docks

### Sunset Coast

**Location.** A small fishing/hunting village in the Avium Savannah, southwest of the
Hunter Guild. [Sunset Coast]

**Reachable by:** Quetzal Transport System, charter ship, boat with Antonia (from
Aldarin), or personal boat at 44 Sailing. [Sunset Coast]

**In sight / nearby buildings:**
- General store
- Thurid's helmet shop (has two anvils inside)
- Picaria's fishing shop
- Multiple cooking ovens
- A sand pit on the village's northern edge
- Fishing spots for sharks (big net / harpoon) right off the coast
[Sunset Coast]

**NPCs actually standing nearby:** village is "inhabited by Fremenniks, who have
travelled from Rellekka to Varlamore" — Thurid (helmet shop owner), Picaria (fishing shop
owner), Reinn, Arnkel, Inga, and Antonia (runs a boat service to Aldarin). A pettable dog,
Molossus, wanders the village. [Sunset Coast]

**Economy.** Fishing (notably sharks), cooking, smithing at the anvils, sand collection.
Nearly no combat threat locally (seagulls, level-2 imps). A transplanted Fremennik fishing
culture, not a native Varlamorian one. [Sunset Coast]

**Charter ship.** Harbor connects to 21 destinations, fares 400–4,500 coins; Cabin Fever
and/or Ring of Charos(a) cut fares up to 50% (stacking to 25%). Aldarin's page separately
notes the short Aldarin↔Sunset Coast hop costs only 500 coins. [Charter ship; Aldarin]

**Sensory details for writers:**
- Salt spray and the smack of nets against the dock pilings
- Wood-smoke and grilling shark from the cooking ovens
- The clang of hammer on anvil drifting from Thurid's shop
- Gulls screaming over discarded fish guts
- A Fremennik accent out of place against the savannah heat — northerners a long way from Rellekka

**Who plausibly hangs around here** (drawn from named NPC types on the page):
1. Fremennik fisherfolk (net/harpoon crews) — know tides, shark runs, boat gossip; don't know Varlamorian court politics or Losanium theology.
2. A helmet-smith's apprentice (Thurid's shop) — knows metal, local smithing trade; not hunting or savannah wildlife.
3. A fishmonger/shop-hand (Picaria's) — knows prices, buyers, which crews are reliable; not inland affairs.
4. A sand-gatherer working the northern pit — knows glassmaking supply chains; little else.
5. A boat-hand or ferry passenger of Antonia's Aldarin run — knows the Aldarin coastal route and gossip carried between the two docks, not Fortis politics.
6. A visiting Hunter Guild trainee passing through en route to the Guild — knows Hunter Guild culture and creatures, only vaguely knows Sunset Coast itself.
7. A retired Fremennik raider/settler among the older villagers — knows Rellekka history and why they left; doesn't know Varlamore's founding myths.
8. A trader crewmember at the charter dock (see section on Trader Crewmember below) — knows fares and destinations, is deliberately incurious about passengers' business.

### Aldarin

**Location.** A large island off Varlamore's south-west coast. Wealthy villa district to
the north/east; "rocky south and west coasts are inhabited by dwarves and bandits."
[Aldarin]

**Buildings/features in sight of the settlement:**
- Alchemical Society Lab (Mastering Mixology minigame)
- Moonrise Brewery and Winery — "the oldest producer of alcohol in Varlamore," over 400 years old, founded by Bracus Ramio, expanded into brewing by his grandson Ludovic [Aldarin; Moonrise Brewery and Winery]
- Villa Lucens, the largest villa, owned by Constantinius
- Mistrock, a mining outpost on the southern coast with a bank
- Sunlight's Sanctum, a pub run by the Moonrise Brewery
- A market with shops and a house portal
[Aldarin]

**NPCs nearby:** Constantinius (villa owner), the Vineyard foreman (oversees grape
harvest), a Vineyard worker, Lucrezia (brewer, "will teach players about brewing"),
Antonius (sommelier/wine merchant), Supervisor Lalo and other Alchemical Society staff.
[Aldarin; Moonrise Brewery and Winery]

**Economy.** Wine and spirits production dominates — the vineyard "rivals that of
Hosidius in scope." Also mining (Mistrock) and alchemy/herblore research (the Lab). A
mixed economy of luxury goods (wine, villas) and working trades (mining, farm labour).
[Aldarin; Moonrise Brewery and Winery]

**Charter ship.** 21 destinations, fares 500–4,500 coins; Sunset Coast is a cheap 500-coin
hop, Civitas illa Fortis is 3,100 coins. [Aldarin]

**Sensory details:**
- Fermenting grape must and oak-barrel must from the brewery's basement, "full of wines in storage"
- Gulls and surf against Mistrock's southern cliffs, grit of stone dust from the mine
- Wealthy perfume and polished stone near Villa Lucens versus sweat and crushed grapes at the vineyard
- Alchemical fumes/odd smells drifting from the Lab
- A sommelier's practiced, unhurried patter at Sunlight's Sanctum

**Who plausibly hangs around here:**
1. A vineyard hand/grape-picker under the foreman — knows grapes, harvest timing, vineyard gossip; doesn't know Fortis court affairs or foreign trade routes.
2. A cooper or cellar worker at the brewery — knows barrels, aging, spirits; not politics.
3. A sommelier's assistant (Antonius's circle) — knows wine quality, wealthy clientele, rumors from villa parties; not military matters.
4. A villa servant (Villa Lucens staff) — knows the habits of the rich, town gossip; sworn to discretion about employers.
5. A Mistrock miner — knows ore, tunnels, the "dwarves and bandits" on the rough coast; not brewing or high society.
6. An Alchemical Society apprentice — knows herblore/mixology theory; oblivious to shipping schedules or dockside talk.
7. A dockside merchant selling produce for charter export — knows fares, cargo, which ships are due; not inland farming.
8. A bandit or rough coastal type from the south/west shore, passing through wary of guards — knows smuggling routes, not palace protocol.

### Fortis Cothon

**Location.** "A large harbour on the north side of Civitas illa Fortis" — the capital's
maritime gateway. [Fortis Cothon]

**Reachable by:** charter ship; after "Children of the Sun," speaking to Regulus Cento
outside Varrock's east gate; personal boat at 38 Sailing. [Fortis Cothon]

**Buildings/features in sight:** gem and fur stalls, silk merchants, bakers, spice
vendors, a general store, a blacksmith, a crafting-supplies shop, and The Flaming Arrow
pub. Connects onward to the Fortis Colosseum, temples, and the Grand Museum in the inner
city. [Fortis Cothon]

**NPCs nearby:** sailors, merchants (gem/fur/silk/spice traders), a bartender,
shopkeepers, guards, craftspeople. Nel, a Knight of Varlamore, is stationed here as part
of the "Twilight's Promise" quest. [Fortis Cothon]

**Economy/character.** The primary trade harbor for the capital — its name references
ancient Phoenician "cothon" ports, and the overall aesthetic leans Roman, matching
Civitas illa Fortis's Perican-derived architecture. [Fortis Cothon; Pericans]

**Society context.** Civitas illa Fortis itself is ruled from the Sunrise Palace by the
Arkan royal family (see Society section below); the Kualti (elite royal guard) are
headquartered near the palace, while ordinary guards patrol the city and Cothon.
[Civitas illa Fortis; Kualti]

**Sensory details:**
- Spice-market smells (pepper, dried fruit) mixed with tar and brine at the wharf
- Hammer-ring from the blacksmith competing with market callers hawking silk and furs
- Baking bread from the bakers' stalls
- Raucous noise spilling from The Flaming Arrow at night
- Roman-style stonework and columns framing the harbor, distinct from Aldarin's rustic-luxury look or Sunset Coast's driftwood-and-nets feel

**Who plausibly hangs around here:**
1. A gem, fur, silk, or spice merchant/stall-hand — knows trade prices and foreign goods, gossip from ship crews; not military or palace matters.
2. A dockworker/sailor loading or unloading charter ships — knows tides, cargo manifests, which captains are trustworthy; not court intrigue.
3. A blacksmith or crafting-supplies seller — knows tools and metalwork; not shipping routes.
4. The Flaming Arrow's bartender or a regular patron — knows tavern rumor, sailors' tall tales; unreliable on hard facts.
5. A city guard patrolling the harbor (distinct from the elite Kualti) — knows local law and troublemakers; not royal family secrets. [Guard (Varlamore)]
6. A Knight of Varlamore posted nearby (per Nel's precedent) — knows chivalric duty and current threats (e.g. Twilight Emissaries); discreet about quest-specific intel. [Fortis Cothon]
7. A trader crewmember at the charter berth — same profile as elsewhere: knows fares/destinations, incurious about passengers. [Trader crewmember]
8. A temple-goer or low clergy member en route to a Ralos temple in the inner city — knows Losanium ritual, not politics. [Ralos]

## 2. Trader Crewmembers (the charter-ship NPC type at all three docks)

Employees of Trader Stan; "Trader crewmembers stand at the docks of each destination in
the charter ships network." They also run "Trader Stan's Trading Post" exotic shop. Known
for flashy, impractical clothing — in-house joke that "Trader Stan makes them wear the
top-of-the-line garments to keep at the height of fashion." Sardonic, quip about quitting
if they fall overboard again. Present at 17+ ports across Gielinor, so a given crewmember
is *not* a Varlamore local — treat them as itinerant, well-traveled, gossip-rich about
other ports but generally not about inland Varlamore affairs. [Trader crewmember; Charter ship]

## 3. Varlamore Society in Brief

**Rulership.** A monarchy: Queen **Zyanyi Arkan**, the "Sun Queen," rules from the
Sunrise Palace in Civitas illa Fortis. Her great-grandfather **Mezpah Arkan** founded the
Arkan dynasty roughly 100 years ago after overthrowing **Emperor Imafore Tullus** of the
preceding Tullus dynasty. She is romantically/ritually paired with **Servius**, the
Teokan (high priest) of Ralos, by "an ancient Varlamorian tradition"; their son is
**Prince Itzla Arkan**, her heir. [Varlamore; Zyanyi Arkan; Ralos]

**Guard/military structure.**
- **Kualti** — the Queen's elite personal guard; headquartered beside the Sunrise Palace in Civitas illa Fortis. Membership requires years of military service plus knight training; initiates are given a ceremonial dagger from a predecessor by the High Priest. Notable member: Yorah (hasta-wielder, they/them). [Kualti]
- **Knights of Varlamore** — general combat NPCs (level 46) found at Kualti Headquarters and Sunrise Palace; Nel (a Knight of Varlamore) is posted at Fortis Cothon during "Twilight's Promise." Distinct from, but overlapping in gear style with, the Kualti. [Knight of Varlamore; Fortis Cothon]
- **Guards (Varlamore)** — ordinary city guards seen in Civitas illa Fortis and at The Teomat; visually unique but functionally standard guards. A **Guard Captain** oversees regular guards in the city. [Guard (Varlamore); Civitas illa Fortis]

**Religion — Losanium.** The primary faith, worshipping sun god **Ralos** and moon god
**Ranul**; Ralos worship is markedly more popular. [Varlamore]
- *Ralos*: sun god, "born from a giant burst of light before the world began"; values boldness, honesty, living fully. Historic (ended) practice of sacrificing criminals at his temples. Sites: Fortis Colosseum (built over an old temple; arena bloodshed treated as an offering), Fortis Temple, and the Teomat atop Ralos' Rise. Clergy: **Teokan** (high priest) — currently **Servius**; **Teoki** (priests, e.g. Cuali, Servius the administrators — see Civitas illa Fortis); **Nelta** (lower clergy, e.g. Manauia). A memorial in the Ruins of Mokhaiotl hints Ralos might be dead. [Ralos]
- *Ranul*: moon/death/knowledge god, "born from the darkness of the night," guides souls through the Gates of Mictl. Sites: the Twilight Temple (built under Emperor Imafore, deemed blasphemous by both faiths, abandoned for centuries, now the Twilight Emissaries' base) and a Crypt of the Moon in Forthos Dungeon. Clergy: Teokan — **Metzli** (resides in the Teomat); Teoki — Seia, Hegio, Ezco, Dominicus, Nenetl; Nelta handle funerary duties. Zuma is a Ranul zealot in "Perilous Moons." [Ranul; Twilight Temple]
- **Twilight Emissaries** — an apocalyptic cult ("Final Dawn") that repurposed the abandoned Twilight Temple; enemies include Emissary Acolytes/Chosen/Ascended and the boss Amoxliatl. [Varlamore; Twilight Temple]

**Main peoples.**
- **Pericans** — fled Great Kourend (via the Custodia Mountains) to escape Xeric; founded Civitas illa Fortis; Roman-derived architecture and Latin-derived names ("Fortis... surrounded by Perican archetecture" — Curator Herminius). The Tullus dynasty was Perican-named. [Pericans]
- **Talkasti** — earlier refugees from the same Age of Strife exodus; initially nomadic, settled the Tlati Rainforest, with Tal Teklan their first major settlement; brought Losanium (secretly practiced back in Kourend) south with them; Nahuatl-derived names; the Arkan dynasty's name itself traces to "early Talkasti nomads." [Talkasti]
- Pericans and Talkasti later intermixed; modern Civitas illa Fortis citizens descend from both, though Perican architecture and governance forms dominate visibly. The River Varla was their old territorial boundary. [Pericans; Talkasti]
- **Imcando dwarves** — founded the underground city of Cam Torum. Modern **Dwarves of Cam Torum** are Imcando descendants who fled Lovakengj when Xeric took power there; they reject the "Imcando" label, are governed independently by **Highlord Katlo** (only Katlo, Attala, and Highseer Vandra may authorize human entry), and view humans as "dangerous, arrogant, and short-sighted." Functionally an independent city-state inside Varlamore's borders, reached via Ralos' Rise. [Varlamore; Cam Torum]
- **Hunter Guild** — not an ethnic group but a major institution: founded anciently as "Cor Venationis" ("Heart of the Hunt"), located in the Avium Savannah just outside Civitas illa Fortis. Leadership: Guildmaster Apatura, Guild Scribe Verity (Hunters' Rumours), Soar Leader Pitri (the Roost / quetzals), shopkeepers Imia and Pellem, and guild hunters Gilman, Ornus, Cervus, Aco, Teco, Wolf, Fox, Kiko. Requires Hunter level 46 (not boostable). [Hunter Guild]

**Outsiders/adventurers.** No single wiki page states an explicit official policy, but
several data points imply the texture: entry to Varlamore proper is gated behind
"Children of the Sun"; Cam Torum's dwarves are openly distrustful of humans and restrict
entry to three named authorities; the Twilight Temple requires literally disguising as a
cult member to get in. The overall picture the wiki paints is a kingdom still cautious
about foreigners, with pockets (Cam Torum, the cult) actively hostile or suspicious, while
the capital's markets and charter docks are otherwise open and mercantile. [Varlamore; Cam Torum; Twilight Temple]

## 4. Travel Inside Varlamore

**Charter ships** connect only the three coastal docks — **Sunset Coast, Aldarin, Civitas
illa Fortis (Fortis Cothon)** — plus the wider Gielinor network; unlocked after
"Children of the Sun." Fares 400–4,500 coins depending on route; Cabin Fever / Ring of
Charos(a) discount up to 50%, stacking to 25%. [Varlamore; Charter ship; Sunset Coast; Aldarin]

**Quetzal Transport System.** Unlocked via the "Twilight's Promise" quest, which gives
the player their own quetzal, **Renu**. Travelled via a "quetzal whistle" or a teleport
spell between built landing sites. 14 total landing sites:
- *Default/built:* Aldarin, Auburnvale, Civitas illa Fortis, Hunter Guild, Quetzacalli Gorge, Sunset Coast, Tal Teklan, The Teomat
- *Buildable:* Cam Torum entrance, Colossal Wyrm Remains, Fortis Colosseum, Kastori, Outer Fortis, Salvager Overlook
Each buildable site costs 10 Quetzal feed + 3 Limestone bricks + 4 Soft clay (~30,330 gp);
all six together ~181,980 gp. Auburnvale's landing spot is "5 tiles from the bank," the
closest of any site. [Quetzal Transport System]

**Other local transport:**
- Antonia runs an informal boat between Sunset Coast and Aldarin. [Sunset Coast]
- Achilka runs a boat service linking Kastori and Tal Teklan. [Kastori; Tal Teklan]
- Fairy ring code **AJP** reaches Locus Oasis. [Locus Oasis]
- **Pendant of Ates** offers teleports to several Varlamore points (Kastori, Cam Torum area/The Darkfrost, Salvager Overlook near Twilight Temple). [Kastori; Cam Torum; Quetzacalli Gorge; Salvager Overlook]
- Calcified moth teleport reaches Cam Torum. [Cam Torum]

A Varlamore-locked player therefore has: charter ship (3 coastal docks only), the full
Quetzal whistle network once unlocked, the fairy ring at Locus Oasis, and the
Pendant of Ates — all of which stay inside the region, so none of them break the
area lock.

## 5. Other Settlements Touched On (for stand-in placement/context)

- **Outer Fortis** — farming satellite south of the capital; growers of onions, wheat, cabbage, sweetcorn (unique here), nettles; a windmill and chicken coop. Shops: Atlazora's Rest (pub), general store, Spike's Spikes (mace shop, run by Spike), Emelio's Kebab Shop (Emelio). Atza is a fur specialist tied to "At First Light." A Ralos temple sits here too. Working-class, rural. [Outer Fortis]
- **Kastori** — rainforest agricultural/fishing village at the south-east edge of Tlati Rainforest; Achilka (boats), Ameyalli (farm supplies), Sulisal (fishing). [Kastori]
- **Cam Torum** — see Society section; independent Imcando-descendant dwarf city, functioning shops (blacksmith, jeweler, herbalist, runic emporium, pickaxe vendor, bakery, the Lost Pickaxe pub). [Cam Torum]
- **Tal Teklan** — Talkasti heartland, first major Talkasti settlement in Tlati Rainforest; NPCs Arcuani (archery), Xochitl (dyes), King (innkeeper), Teicuh (runes). [Tal Teklan]
- **Auburnvale** — town in the regrowing Auburn Valley (devastated in the "War of Betrayal," now tended by animal spirits and Ents); Lunami (axes), Sebamo (staffs), Chouani (tannery); sawmill next to the bank. [Auburnvale]
- **Locus Oasis** — oasis in the Avium Savannah; Marcellus (farm manager) plus workers Cuthbert, Dave, Gary, Jane, Sue; setting for "The Ribbiting Tale of a Lily Pad Labour Dispute." [Locus Oasis]
- **Quetzacalli Gorge** — frontier mountain settlement in the Hailstorm Mountains, north of the Fortis aqueduct; bank, general store, The Windbreaker pub; alpaca and a pettable dog, Chiribaya. [Quetzacalli Gorge]
- **Ralos' Rise** — the mountain holding the Teomat (joint home of the Church of Ralos and Sect of Ranul), plus the entrances to Cam Torum and Neypotzli; considered the site of Ralos's "first and only appearance." Feeds the Fortis Aqueduct, the capital's fresh water. [Ralos' Rise]
- **Neypotzli** — underground prison beneath Cam Torum built by "the Old Ones" to contain the Moons of Peril; guarded/serviced by Eyatlalli, a "nagua of earth and stream"; Jessamine leads an archaeological dig here from Civitas illa Fortis. [Neypotzli]
- **Salvager Overlook** — small village north of the capital in the Hailstorm Mountains, between Quetzacalli Gorge and the Twilight Temple; led by Izel, with Hugh, Jasper, and Salius (armour shop). [Salvager Overlook]

## 6. Glossary (Varlamorian words, Nahuatl-inspired)

Per the wiki's dedicated language notes: "context clues are used to infer their meaning"
since no official translations exist; vocabulary is "loosely based on the real-world
Nahuatl language." [Varlamorian language glossary page]

| Word | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Nilsal** | Greeting — "hello"/"greetings" | from Nahuatl *niltze* |
| **Timoiva** | Farewell — "goodbye," "see you around" | from Nahuatl *timo-itazque* |
| **Iknami** | Term of endearment — "my friend" | from Nahuatl *icniuhtli* ('brother, friend') |
| **Kuani** | Exclamation — "excellent" | from Nahuatl *cualli* ('good') |
| **Tetamo** | Frustration/disbelief — "oh no," "damn it" | Varlamorian-original coinage |

Other recurring native-language terms seen across the settlement/religion pages (not
translated on a glossary page, but consistently used as titles/proper nouns): **Teokan**
(high priest), **Teoki** (priest/regional administrator), **Nelta** (lower clergy),
**Teomat** (the Ralos/Ranul temple complex on Ralos' Rise), **Kualti** (royal elite
guard), **Cor Venationis** (Latin, "Heart of the Hunt" — the Hunter Guild's old name).
[Ralos; Ranul; Kualti; Hunter Guild]
