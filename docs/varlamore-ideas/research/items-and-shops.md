# Varlamore Items & Shops Research (for Varlamore-locked UIM)

Researched via the OSRS Wiki (oldschool.runescape.wiki), September 2026. Every fact below is
cited to a wiki page. Anything not directly confirmed on a fetched page is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
All shop stock tables reflect stock *as documented on the wiki* — actual live prices/stock fluctuate
with GE-style shop mechanics, irrelevant to a no-trade UIM.

## 3. Unobtainable essentials (no Varlamore source found)

| Item | Why it matters | Closest substitute in-region |
|---|---|---|
| Holy symbol | Prayer-training tool / god item | None found; Temple of Ralos (Civitas illa Fortis) is a *worship altar*, not a symbol seller — [Temple (Civitas illa Fortis)](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Temple_\(Civitas_illa_Fortis\)) was not checked for symbol sales, flagged UNVERIFIED |
| Amulet of glory | Fast teleport/skill-boost jewelry; irrelevant for teleport-blocking plugin's philosophy anyway | None — the plugin's premise (no teleports) makes this moot rather than a gap |
| Rune essence / Pure essence | Runecrafting | None; a Varlamore-locked UIM cannot train Runecrafting via essence mining in-region — must rely on shop-bought runes (Tal Teklan Rune Shop, Runic Emporium) for magic |

| Cooking gauntlets | Reduces burn rate on lobster+ | None confirmed; UNVERIFIED whether any Varlamore quest/activity grants them |
| Snape grass | Prayer potion secondary | None found; a UIM is limited to Mastering Mixology potion packs for finished Prayer potions instead of brewing their own |
| Battlestaves | Magic weapon | Sebamo's Sublime Staffs explicitly does **not** stock them ("ents mistaking a delivery cart as a gift") |

## 5. Ideas hooks — 10 "key item" milestones for a progress tracker

1. **First knife** — buyable from any of 7 Varlamore general stores (e.g. Fortis General Store, Civitas illa Fortis Bazaar) for 7 gp. Source: [Fortis General Store](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fortis_General_Store).
2. **First pickaxe (bronze)** — buyable from Cam Torum/Kastori/Sunset Coast/Tal Teklan general stores or Mistrock Mining Supplies/Tizoro's Pickaxes. Source: [Mistrock Mining Supplies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mistrock_Mining_Supplies).
3. **First tiered pickaxe (iron→rune)** — only fully available from **Tizoro's Pickaxes, Cam Torum** — flag as the region's single full-range pickaxe vendor. Source: [Tizoro's Pickaxes](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tizoro%27s_Pickaxes).
4. **First bow + arrows** — only sold at **Arcuani's Archery Supplies, Tal Teklan** — losing access here (e.g. before unlocking Tal Teklan) blocks all shop-bought ranged gear. Source: [Arcuani's Archery Supplies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Arcuani%27s_Archery_Supplies).
5. **First glassblowing pipe / shears** — single spawn point in the whole region: **Artima's Crafting Supplies, Civitas illa Fortis Bazaar**. A tracker milestone "Crafting unlocked" should hinge on this one shop. Source: [Artima's Crafting Supplies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Artima%27s_Crafting_Supplies).
6. **First saw** — single Varlamore source: **the Auburnvale Sawmill** (13 gp), which doubles as the region's most efficient plank-making sawmill. Source: [Sawmill](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Sawmill).
7. **Farming toolkit complete** — rake, seed dibber, secateurs, watering can, and gardening trowel are ALL single-sourced at **Kastori Farming Supplies**; a UIM who hasn't reached Kastori has zero shop-bought farming tools anywhere else in Varlamore. Source: [Kastori Farming Supplies](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Kastori_Farming_Supplies).
8. **Quetzal whistle crafted** — gated behind 10 Hunters' Rumours (Hunter Guild) + 1 willow log + knife; the tracker could show "Quetzal Transport unlocked" as a distinct milestone from merely reaching the Hunter Guild. Source: [Basic quetzal whistle](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Basic_quetzal_whistle).
9. **Pendant of Ates obtained** — this is a rare combat drop (Frost Nagua 1/100, Amoxliatl 1/25), not a shop item; a milestone here should show as "found," not "bought," and separately track "statues activated" since it starts inert. Source: [Pendant of Ates](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pendant_of_Ates).
10. **First Prayer potion / Antipoison** — Varlamore has **no direct shop seller** for either; both come only from Mastering Mixology potion packs (Aldarin, requires 60 Herblore + Children of the Sun). A tracker should flag "Prayer restoration access" as minigame-gated rather than assume UIMs can just walk into a shop. Source: [Mastering Mixology](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mastering_Mixology).
